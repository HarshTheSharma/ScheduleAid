Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026  ·  ScheduleAI'd

This Privacy Policy explains how ScheduleAI'd ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

ScheduleAI'd does not operate a backend server and does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server we control. All data stays on your device.

The following information is stored locally in your browser's localStorage:

  • Your Google OAuth access token and its expiry time, used to authenticate requests to Google Calendar on your behalf.
  • Your Gemini API key, used to make requests to the Gemini API on your behalf.

This data never leaves your browser except as part of direct API calls to Google's services (described below).

2. Third-Party Services

ScheduleAI'd integrates with the following third-party services. Your use of the app is subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Calendar API — your calendar data is accessed directly from your browser using your OAuth token. We do not proxy, store, or log any calendar data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API — your natural language requests and calendar context are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API using your API key. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google OAuth — sign-in is handled via Google's OAuth 2.0 flow. We only request the calendar scope necessary for the app to function.

3. Cookies

ScheduleAI'd does not use cookies. Authentication state is stored in localStorage only.

4. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring tools. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

5. Children's Privacy

ScheduleAI'd is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, please contact us.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no data held by us to access, correct, or delete. To remove all locally stored data, clear your browser's localStorage for this site or sign out of the app.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). As we collect no personal data on our end, these rights are exercised directly with Google for data processed by their services.

7. Data Security

Your API key and OAuth token are stored only in your browser's localStorage. You are responsible for the security of your own device and browser. We strongly recommend signing out when using shared devices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at HarshSharma.dev.
